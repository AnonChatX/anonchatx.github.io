---
title: What is AnonChatX?
description: Private chat over Tor. Minimal metadata. Open source.
---

AnonChatX is a privacy-focused messaging app designed to work over Tor (including bridges).

## What AnonChatX is for
- Communicating when networks are restricted
- Minimizing metadata exposure
- Verifiable, open-source distribution

## What AnonChatX is not
- A guarantee of anonymity if your device is compromised
- A replacement for good operational security
